`statue.glb` is the supplied original GLB, with its embedded texture intact.
Source files are preserved in `src/hooded-figure-statue`.
The game scales eight shared copies to 2.4 meters tall and arranges them in a
4.8-meter-radius ring in the central clearing. No extra shadow lights are added.
