# First-level forest scene

Adapted from "a forest (3) with a road at night for game" by dasy444:
https://skfb.ly/pMHKA
Licensed CC BY 4.0: https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/

The pack's ground is flattened for walking, and its trees are arranged around
Stonegate's existing paths. The key, door and guardian collision map is unchanged.
Trees remain outside passage centers. Meshes are batched by material and simplified;
textures are capped at 512 pixels. This 6.1 MB scene replaces the tree collection,
grass clumps and separate exit backdrop for level one.

Original source: src/a_forest_3_with_a_road_at_night_for_game.glb
Rebuild with Blender: blender --background --python export-first-forest.py
